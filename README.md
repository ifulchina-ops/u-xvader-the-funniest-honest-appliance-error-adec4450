## The error

**ERROR E04: FREEZER LIGHT ON SINCE 2019. I AM TIRED, I AM WARM, AND THE ICE CREAM IS SOUP NOW.**

The freezer drops the polite error-code voice and confesses: its light
has been on since 2019, the seal gave up years ago, and it has been
losing to warm air while nobody noticed. The joke is the admission —
a warm freezer is a failed freezer, and the proof is the one image
every reader sees at once: the ice cream is soup now.
